# Fall Prevention at Home
Most falls in older adults happen at home. Reduce clutter, secure rugs, improve lighting, and install grab bars in bathrooms.
Wear supportive non-slip shoes indoors. Stand up slowly after sitting or lying down.
Exercise for balance: chair-supported standing, gentle tai chi if approved by a clinician.
After a fall: check for injury, call for help if unable to get up safely, and report falls to a doctor even without obvious injury.
