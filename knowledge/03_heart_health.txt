# Heart Health for Seniors
Heart-healthy habits include light daily activity, a diet rich in vegetables and whole grains, not smoking, and controlling blood pressure and cholesterol.
Warning signs of a possible heart attack: chest pressure, pain radiating to arm/jaw, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea.
Call emergency services immediately for suspected heart attack. Do not drive yourself.
Cardiac rehab and gentle walking programs can improve recovery after clinician approval.
