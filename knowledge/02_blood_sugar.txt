# Blood Sugar and Diabetes Care
Typical fasting blood sugar targets for many adults with diabetes are individualized; common clinic targets are often near 80-130 mg/dL before meals.
Hypoglycemia signs: sweating, shaking, confusion, hunger, dizziness. Treat with fast sugar if conscious and protocol allows, then recheck.
Hyperglycemia signs: thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision.
Tips: eat regular meals, take medicine as prescribed, stay hydrated, and carry a glucose source if advised by a clinician.
Older adults should never stop diabetes medicine without medical advice.
