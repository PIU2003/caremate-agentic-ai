# Blood Pressure in Older Adults
Normal blood pressure for many older adults is around 120/80 mmHg, but targets may be individualized by a doctor.
High blood pressure (hypertension) often has no symptoms and increases stroke and heart disease risk.
Lifestyle support: reduce salt, stay active with light walking, limit alcohol, manage stress, and take prescribed medicine on time.
Home monitoring: sit quietly for 5 minutes, use a validated cuff, measure at the same time daily, and record readings for the clinician.
Seek urgent care for sudden severe headache, chest pain, confusion, or blood pressure readings far above the usual range with symptoms.
