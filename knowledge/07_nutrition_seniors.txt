# Nutrition for Older Adults
Aim for balanced plates: vegetables, protein (fish, eggs, beans, lean meat), whole grains, and healthy fats.
Protein helps preserve muscle. Calcium and vitamin D support bones; discuss supplements with a clinician.
Drink water regularly; thirst sensation may decline with age.
Limit ultra-processed foods high in salt and sugar. Soft, easy-to-chew options help if dental issues exist.
